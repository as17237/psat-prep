# PSAT Prep v2.4.0

Released 2026-09-10. Production deployment completed from the tested `5e0e963`
candidate after backup, integrity, restore, and `/v2/` smoke gates passed.

## What changed

- Added one shared sync coordinator for student, parent, and mistakes pages.
- Preserved pending writes across downloads, reloads, offline periods, and transient
  failures; uploads retry until acknowledged without dropping later edits.
- Added independent revision ordering for bookmarks and error tags on the client and
  API, including deliberate bookmark removals and tag resolution after later answers.
- Added pause/resume for timed exams. Remaining time is banked, deadlines are removed
  while paused, answering is blocked, reloads preserve the pause, and reports record
  pause count and time away.
- Added short-test scaled estimates requiring 15 answered questions per section and
  30 total. Student and parent reports share the same estimate, disclosure, and
  provenance; focused estimates stay out of full-exam trends.
- Added domain-focused and time/question-count test validation coverage, offline
  completion and lost-ack replay coverage, and storage-recovery regressions.
- Consolidated current setup and recovery documentation and removed superseded handoff
  documents while retaining rollback and source-PDF recovery references.
