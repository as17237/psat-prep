Linear Functions & Systems

Slope Formula: m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1)
Slope-Intercept & Point-Slope: y = mx + b • y - y1 = m(x - x1)
Parallel & Perpendicular Lines: Parallel: m1 = m2 • Perp: m1 × m2 = -1
System Solutions (Ax + By = C): 1 Sol: diff slopes • No Sol: same slope, diff y-int • ∞ Sol: identical

Quadratic Functions & Parabolas

Quadratic Formula & Vertex: x = (-b ± √(b2 - 4ac)) / (2a)
Vertex x = -b / (2a) • y = a(x - h)2 + k
Discriminant (b2 - 4ac):
> 0: 2 real roots • = 0: 1 real root • < 0: 0 real roots
Sum of roots = -b/a • Product of roots = c/a

Exponents, Radicals & Growth Models

Exponent Rules: xa×xb = xa+b • (xa)b = xab
x-a = 1/xa • x0 = 1
Fractional Powers: xa/b = b√(xa) = (b√x)a
Exponential Growth & Decay: y = a(1 ± r)t
a=initial, r=rate, t=time

Ratios, Proportions & Percent Change

Percent Change: % Change = ((New - Old) / Old) × 100%
Percent Multiplier: Increase: (1 + r) • Decrease: (1 - r)

Coordinate Geometry & Circle Equations

Distance & Midpoint: d = √((x2 - x1)2 + (y2 - y1)2)
Midpoint: ((x1+x2)/2, (y1+y2)/2)
Standard Circle Equation: (x - h)2 + (y - k)2 = r2
Center at (h, k), Radius = r

Statistics & Probability

Mean & Median: Mean = (∑ x) / n
Median: Middle ranked value
Range & Std Dev: Range = Max - Min
Standard Dev: Spread around mean
Basic Probability: P(E) = Favorable / Total
